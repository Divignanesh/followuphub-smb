# FollowUpHub for small business: layout (v5)

Same product as the real estate site, for Canadian owner-operators: trades,
clinics, salons, auto shops, studios.

## Sources

- **Colours: Nitux** (Ofspace, dribbble.com/shots/26138549). Indigo `#5755dd`
  for actions, lavender `#f4edfb` into pink `#f3c8d7` inside panels.
- **Layout and components: Flowa** (Vektora, dribbble.com/shots/27690000).
- **Base: white.** No canvas frame around the page; colour lives inside
  panels only (user direction, 2026-10-30).

## Copy rules (user, 2026-10-30)

No numbers and no claims on the page: no statistics, percentages, ROI or
example dollar amounts. Keep it subtle and informative about what the
product does. Plan prices stay in the pricing section only, as terms.

## Hero copy

> [pill] The front desk for Canadian small businesses
> Turn every missed call into a booked job
> FollowUpHub texts back, answers questions, books the work and sends the
> invoice, while you stay on the job.
