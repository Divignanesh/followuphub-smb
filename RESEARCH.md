# Canadian small business: research behind the page

Gathered October 2026. [CA] Canadian source, [US] US source, [UNVERIFIED] widely repeated but no primary source found.

## Who they are
- 1,079,188 small employer businesses (1-99 staff), 98.2% of all employers; 59.1% have 1-4 employees. [CA] ISED, Key Small Business Statistics 2025.
- Small firms employ 5.8M people, 46.6% of the private workforce. [CA] ISED 2025.
- 735 hours a year on compliance, 256 of them red tape; firms under 5 staff pay $10,208 per employee. [CA] CFIB Red Tape Report 2025.
- Top concerns (Sept 2025): taxes 63%, operating costs 60%, uncertainty 54%. [CA] CFIB Your Voice.
- 32% have already moved to Canadian suppliers; about half no longer see the US as reliable. [CA] CFIB 2025-2026.

## Calls and leads
- Contacted within an hour: nearly 7x more likely to qualify; 23% of firms never replied. [US] HBR, Oldroyd et al., 2011. **Used on the page.**
- 21% of callers who reach no one immediately call another business. [US] CallRail 2025. **Used on the page.**
- 62% of calls to small businesses go unanswered. [US, 85 businesses] Not used.
- "85% never call back": no primary source. Not used.
- 83% read Google reviews; 89% expect owners to reply. [US/global] BrightLocal 2025.
